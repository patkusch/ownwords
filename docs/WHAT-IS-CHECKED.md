# What the lock checks

The lock compares the original with the rewrite. It reports each of these if it went missing or appeared.

## Facts

- Numbers, including "12%", "$3 million" and numbers written as words
- Dates
- Times
- Links
- Email addresses
- Citations
- Quotations, word for word
- Names

## Claims

- A word of doubt was removed, such as "may" or "suggests". The claim is now stronger.
- A word such as "about" or "at least" was removed. A figure is now exact.
- A word such as "some" or "often" was removed.
- A word of certainty was added, such as "definitely".
- The number of "not" words changed. A meaning may have flipped.

## Size

- The text got more than a fifth longer.
- More than half the words are gone.
- There are noticeably more sentences than before.

## What comes back

Each finding is marked "fail" or "review". One "fail" fails the rewrite. A "review" means no fact changed, but a person should look.

The report also says whether the rewrite came out plainer or heavier. That is a note, not a pass or a fail.

## What it does not check

It does not understand meaning. The blind spots are measured in the [README](../README.md#what-the-checker-catches).
