## Tidepool - Fast Photo Folder Sync

**Tidepool** is a lightweight, open-source tool written in Go that syncs your photo folders. Version 2.3 was released on May 5th, 2026, with improved performance and reliability.

Tidepool saves you transferring photos between devices by hand. It keeps your galleries consistent across all your locations.

**Key Features:**

* Version 2.3 is approximately three times as fast as previous versions (based on author's internal testing).
* Successfully tested with over 40,000 files.
* Easy to configure and use.
* Built in Go, with performance and efficiency in mind.

**Important Note:** Our testing shows significant speed improvements, but performance may vary depending on disk speed.
