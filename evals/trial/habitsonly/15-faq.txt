**Q: Is the Oakline water filter safe for my well water?**

A: The Oakline filter is designed to improve the taste and odor of your water, but whether it suits well water depends on your specific water quality. Our lab testing, conducted by Hartwell Labs in 2025, demonstrates its effectiveness in removing contaminants.

The Oakline filter removes at least 99% of lead and chlorine, common issues found in well water. But it has limits. **It does not remove bacteria or nitrates.**

Before you use it:

*   You *must* have your well water professionally tested first. The test will identify the specific contaminants present and determine if the Oakline filter is sufficient.
*   A single Oakline cartridge filters approximately 400 litres (or lasts around 2 months) depending on water usage.
*   Currently, the Oakline filter is **not certified** for use with well water.

**Disclaimer:** The Oakline filter is intended for improving the taste and odor of water and should be used in conjunction with professional water testing and appropriate follow-up treatment if needed.
