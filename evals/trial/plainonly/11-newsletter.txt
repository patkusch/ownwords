## Autumn Sale a Huge Success for Ashby Road Garden!

Over 150 community members came to our Autumn Sale on October 18th to support the Ashby Road Garden. We raised **£1,340**, thanks to everyone’s generosity and support.

A huge thank you to our dedicated volunteers, particularly Ruth and Imran, who worked tirelessly to build six new raised beds. The beds significantly expand our growing space. We also have a brand-new water butt, which now holds 1,000 litres of water for our plants.

However, we face a potential challenge. We’ve been told that the council may reduce our grant funding next year, with a decision expected in January. We’ll keep a close eye on developments and look at all options to make sure the garden continues to flourish.

Let’s keep the momentum going!
