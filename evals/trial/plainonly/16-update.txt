Subject: Canal Street Bridge Repair Project Update – October 26, 2023

Dear Stakeholders,

This is a progress report on the Canal Street Bridge repair project. As of today, October 26th, the project is 62% complete. However, an unforeseen delay has affected the originally scheduled completion date of September 30th, 2026.

We now expect to finish in late November. The main cause is a critical delay in the delivery of steel components: the shipment arrived five weeks behind schedule.

The project budget is now £3.4 million and spending totals £2.3 million. We are closely monitoring material costs and working with suppliers to limit further disruption.

Our engineering team, led by Fatima Noor, is working hard to speed up the remaining tasks. We’ve made excellent progress, but heavy rainfall could potentially add another week of delay. We are closely monitoring weather forecasts.

There have been no reported injuries on site, and safety remains our top priority. We will continue to send regular updates and will tell you immediately of any significant developments.
