## Dunmore Plumbing: A Cautious Embrace of Online Booking

**Case Study: March 2026**

Dunmore Plumbing is a family-run firm that employs 11 people and is run by its owner, Colm Dunmore. It started using an online booking system on March 1st, 2026, to simplify service scheduling and make booking more convenient for customers. Before this, Colm and his team relied solely on phone calls for appointment requests, a process that was proving increasingly inefficient.

Colm met the initial rollout with some skepticism and raised concerns about the fees. The system was activated anyway. It gives customers 24/7 access to view available slots and book appointments.

The impact showed within the first month. Missed calls fell from approximately 40 per week to 12. However, no-shows remained consistent, which suggests the booking system hadn’t fundamentally altered customer behaviour.

Perhaps the most significant change was the rise in bookings outside standard office hours, which now account for 28% of the total bookings. Colm remains cautiously optimistic. He said, “I was sceptical and I am still not sure about the fees," so the system’s long-term value and potential return on investment still need to be assessed.

**Key Takeaway:** Dunmore Plumbing saw a clear improvement in how efficiently it manages appointments. But the owner's initial reservations show the importance of careful cost-benefit analysis when adopting new technologies.
