**Q: Is the Oakline water filter safe for my well water?**

A: The Oakline filter is designed to improve the taste and odor of your water. Whether it suits well water depends on your specific water quality. Our rigorous lab testing, carried out by Hartwell Labs in 2025, demonstrates that it is effective at removing contaminants.

The Oakline filter removes at least 99% of lead and chlorine, which are common problems in well water. **It does not remove bacteria or nitrates.**

* Before using the Oakline filter, you *must* have your well water professionally tested. This will identify the specific contaminants present and show whether the Oakline filter is enough.
* A single Oakline cartridge filters approximately 400 litres (or lasts around 2 months) depending on water usage.
* The Oakline filter is currently **not certified** for use with well water.

**Disclaimer:** The Oakline filter is intended for improving the taste and odor of water and should be used in conjunction with professional water testing and appropriate follow-up treatment if needed.
