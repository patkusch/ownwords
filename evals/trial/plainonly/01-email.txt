Subject: Update on Boiler Replacement & Heating Issues at Harlow Street

Dear Harlow Street Tenants’ Group,

This is an update on the boiler replacement that Kessler Heating carried out on October 14th, 2025. As you know, this project cost £8,450.

Unfortunately, we’ve had significant problems with the new boiler. Initial reports indicate that 23 out of 31 flats had no heating for six days after the installation. We’re investigating the root cause and working with Kessler Heating to make sure this doesn’t repeat.

The landlord is looking at whether a partial refund to the service charge is possible, to make up for the inconvenience and disruption. However, nothing has been formally confirmed yet and we’re waiting for a final assessment.

We’ll keep you updated. If you have any questions or concerns, please contact me directly at repairs@harlowstreet.example.com.
