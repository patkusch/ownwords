**MEMORANDUM**

**TO:** Head of IT Operations
**FROM:** [Your Name/Department]
**DATE:** 26 October 2026
**SUBJECT:** Recommendation: Trial of Remote First-Line IT Support

**Executive Summary:** This memo recommends a 12-week trial of remote first-line IT support, to deal with rising help-desk ticket volumes and improve response times.

**Background:** Our help-desk team of three handles 1,840 tickets per quarter. Password resets account for approximately 70% of requests. This results in significant wait times, which affect user productivity.

**Proposed Solution:** We propose a 12-week trial of a remote support model, starting January 11, 2027. A successful trial at the Leeds office in 2025 reduced average wait time from 22 minutes to 9. Using what was learned there, we believe this approach can significantly improve efficiency.

**Financial Considerations:** The estimated cost of this trial, including software and training, is under £5,000.

**Recommendation:** We strongly recommend starting this trial to assess whether remote first-line support is feasible in our environment and what impact it has. Success will be measured by reduced average ticket resolution time and overall user satisfaction.

**Next Steps:** We will write a detailed plan for carrying out the trial and get stakeholder approval to start it.
