**The Riverside Book Festival Returns!**

Join us for the 7th annual Riverside Book Festival in beautiful Shrewsbury from June 12th to 14th, 2026. We welcome over 34 authors, including bestselling novelists Nadia Halabi and Tom Reece.

Discover your next favourite read and meet your favourite authors.

Festival details:

* Dates: June 12th - 14th, 2026
* Location: Shrewsbury
* Authors: Featuring Nadia Halabi, Tom Reece & many more
* Tickets: Weekend pass – £45, Day ticket – £18, Under 16s free
* Saturday evening: a special talk starting at 19:30

Last year’s festival attracted over 2,600 book lovers.
