**Elm Park Primary Reading Programme – Interim Report (September 2025 - March 2026)**

This report gives the interim findings of the school-led reading programme run at Elm Park Primary between September 2025 and March 2026 for pupils in Years 3 and 4 (n=112). The programme cost £14 per pupil. It focused on improved literacy support and targeted intervention strategies.

Initial results show promising progress across the cohort. Analysis indicates an average rise in reading age of 7 months, which reflects the impact of the focused support. However, 18 pupils made no measurable progress. This shows the need for a nuanced understanding of individual learning needs.

Headteacher Joan Whitlock has noted “early signs are encouraging,” but also stressed the need to keep collecting data. She believes further analysis is crucial, particularly of the reasons behind the 18 pupils’ lack of progress.

From now on, we will keep monitoring pupil progress closely, using formative assessments and adapting our approach where necessary. A full evaluation will be carried out after the programme is completed in March 2026. It will look more deeply at the factors influencing outcomes, to give a fuller assessment of the programme’s overall effectiveness.
