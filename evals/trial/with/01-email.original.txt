Subject: Update on Boiler Replacement & Heating Issues at Harlow Street

Dear Harlow Street Tenants’ Group,

I’m writing to provide an update following the recent boiler replacement on October 14th, 2025, carried out by Kessler Heating. As you know, this project cost £8,450.

Unfortunately, we’ve experienced significant issues with the new boiler. Initial reports indicate that 23 out of 31 flats experienced a complete lack of heating for six days following the installation. We’re actively investigating the root cause of this disruption and working with Kessler Heating to ensure this doesn’t repeat.

The landlord is currently exploring the possibility of a partial refund to the service charge to compensate for the inconvenience and disruption caused. However, at this time, nothing has been formally confirmed and we’re awaiting a final assessment. 

We’ll continue to keep you updated on our progress. Please don't hesitate to contact me directly at repairs@harlowstreet.example.com if you have any questions or concerns.
