**Leeds Bakery: Four Days a Week – Is It Working?**

Marta Kowalczyk, owner of Marta’s Bakery in Leeds, switched her team to a four-day week in February 2026. Starting her business on the 2nd, she initially employed 9 staff and experienced a slight dip in sales of around 4% in the first month. Things quickly rebounded.

Marta has also been tracking the wellbeing of her team. Two staff members have reported significantly improved sleep patterns.

“It’s been incredible to see the difference,” Marta told us. “The staff feel more energized and focused.”

However, Marta admits she’s facing a significant hurdle: the upcoming Christmas rush. With increased demand, she’s questioning whether a four-day week can sustain the bakery’s operations. “We’re managing, but I’m worried about the pressure,” she said.
