## Dunmore Plumbing: A Cautious Embrace of Online Booking

**Case Study: March 2026**

Dunmore Plumbing is a family-run firm that employs 11 people and is run by its owner, Colm Dunmore. It introduced an online booking system on March 1st, 2026, to simplify scheduling and make booking more convenient for customers. Before this, Colm and his team relied solely on phone calls for appointment requests, which was proving increasingly inefficient.

Colm met the initial rollout with some skepticism and had concerns about the fees. The system went live despite this, giving customers 24/7 access to view available slots and book appointments.

Within the first month, missed calls fell from approximately 40 per week to 12. No-shows remained consistent, which suggests the booking system hadn’t fundamentally altered customer behaviour.

Perhaps the most significant change was the rise in bookings outside of standard office hours, which now account for 28% of the total bookings. Colm remains cautiously optimistic, stating, “I was sceptical and I am still not sure about the fees," 

**Key Takeaway:** Dunmore Plumbing saw a clear improvement in appointment management, but the owner’s doubts about fees mean the system’s long-term value and return on investment still need to be assessed.
