**MEMORANDUM**

**TO:** Head of IT Operations
**FROM:** [Your Name/Department]
**DATE:** 26 October 2026
**SUBJECT:** Recommendation: Trial of Remote First-Line IT Support

**Executive Summary:** This memo recommends a 12-week trial of remote first-line IT support to address increasing help-desk ticket volumes and improve response times. 

**Background:** Our current help-desk team of three handles 1,840 tickets per quarter, with password resets accounting for approximately 70% of requests. This results in significant wait times, impacting user productivity.

**Proposed Solution:** We propose a 12-week trial, commencing January 11, 2027, utilizing a remote support model. Leveraging learnings from a successful trial at the Leeds office in 2025 – which reduced average wait time from 22 minutes to 9 – we believe this approach can significantly improve efficiency. 

**Financial Considerations:** The estimated cost for this trial, including software and training, is under £5,000. 

**Recommendation:** We strongly recommend initiating this trial to assess the feasibility and impact of remote first-line support within our environment. Success will be measured by reduced average ticket resolution time and overall user satisfaction. 

**Next Steps:** We will develop a detailed implementation plan and secure stakeholder approval for the trial commencement.
