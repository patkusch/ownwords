Subject: Canal Street Bridge Repair Project Update – October 26, 2023

Dear Stakeholders,

As of today, October 26th, the Canal Street Bridge repair project is 62% complete. However, an unforeseen delay has affected the originally scheduled completion date of September 30th, 2026.

We now anticipate completion in late November. The main cause is a shipment of steel components that arrived five weeks behind schedule.

The project budget stands at £3.4 million, with expenditure totaling £2.3 million. We are closely monitoring material costs and working with suppliers to mitigate further disruption.

Our engineering team, led by Fatima Noor, is working to accelerate the remaining tasks. Heavy rainfall could potentially add another week of delay, and we are closely monitoring weather forecasts.

There have been no reported injuries on site. We will continue to provide regular updates and will notify you immediately of any significant developments.
