**MEMORANDUM**

**TO:** Head of IT Operations
**FROM:** [Your Name/Department]
**DATE:** 26 October 2026
**SUBJECT:** Recommendation: Trial of Remote First-Line IT Support

We strongly recommend a 12-week trial of remote first-line IT support, starting January 11, 2027, to address increasing help-desk ticket volumes and improve response times.

Our current help-desk team of three handles 1,840 tickets per quarter. Password resets account for approximately 70% of requests. The result is significant wait times, which affect user productivity.

A successful trial at the Leeds office in 2025 reduced average wait time from 22 minutes to 9. We believe the same approach can significantly improve efficiency here, and the trial would show whether it is feasible in our environment.

The estimated cost for this trial, including software and training, is under £5,000.

We will measure success by reduced average ticket resolution time and overall user satisfaction.

Next, we will develop a detailed implementation plan and secure stakeholder approval to start the trial.
