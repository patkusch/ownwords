**Elm Park Primary Reading Programme – Interim Report (September 2025 - March 2026)**

This report gives the interim findings of the school-led reading programme run at Elm Park Primary between September 2025 and March 2026 for pupils in Years 3 and 4 (n=112). The programme cost £14 per pupil and focused on enhanced literacy support and targeted intervention strategies.

Initial results are promising across the cohort. Analysis indicates an average rise in reading age of 7 months. However, 18 pupils made no measurable progress.

Headteacher Joan Whitlock has noted “early signs are encouraging,” but also said that data collection needs to continue. She believes further analysis, particularly of the reasons behind the 18 pupils’ lack of progress, is crucial.

We will continue to monitor pupil progress closely, using formative assessments and adapting our approach where necessary. A full evaluation, looking more closely at the factors influencing outcomes, will be carried out after the programme is completed in March 2026.
