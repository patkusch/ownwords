## Tidepool - Fast Photo Folder Sync

Tidepool is a lightweight, open-source tool written in Go that syncs your photo folders. It automates transferring photos between devices and keeps your galleries consistent across all your locations.

Version 2.3 was released on May 5th, 2026, with improved performance and reliability.

* Version 2.3 is approximately three times as fast as previous versions (based on author's internal testing). Performance may vary depending on disk speed.
* Tested with over 40,000 files.
* Easy to configure and use.
