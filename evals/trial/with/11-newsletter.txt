## Autumn Sale a Success for Ashby Road Garden

Over 150 community members came to our Autumn Sale on October 18th to support the Ashby Road Garden. We raised **£1,340**. Thank you to everyone for your generosity and support.

A huge thank you to our volunteers, particularly Ruth and Imran, who built six new raised beds and significantly expanded our growing space. We also have a new water butt, which holds 1,000 litres of water.

We’ve been informed that the council may reduce our grant funding next year, with a decision expected in January. We’ll keep a close eye on developments and explore all options to keep the garden going.
