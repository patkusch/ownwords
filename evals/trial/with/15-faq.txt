**Q: Is the Oakline water filter safe for my well water?**

A: That depends on your specific water quality. The Oakline filter is designed to improve the taste and odor of your water. Currently, it is **not certified** for use with well water.

In lab testing conducted by Hartwell Labs in 2025, the filter removed at least 99% of lead and chlorine, which are common issues in well water. **It does not remove bacteria or nitrates.**

Before using the Oakline filter, you *must* have your well water professionally tested. The test will identify the specific contaminants present and determine whether the Oakline filter is sufficient. Follow up with appropriate treatment if needed.

A single Oakline cartridge filters approximately 400 litres (or lasts around 2 months) depending on water usage.
