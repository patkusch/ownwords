**Leeds Bakery Takes a Bold Step: Four Days a Week – Is It Working?**

Marta Kowalczyk, owner of the charming Marta’s Bakery in Leeds, took a gamble in February 2026 – switching her team to a four-day week. Starting her business on the 2nd, she initially employed 9 staff and experienced a slight dip in sales of around 4% in the first month. Thankfully, things quickly rebounded!

But the real story isn’t just about the numbers. Marta’s been tracking the wellbeing of her team, and the results are fascinating. Two staff members have reported significantly improved sleep patterns – a huge benefit for anyone working long hours. 

“It’s been incredible to see the difference,” Marta told us. “The staff feel more energized and focused.” 

However, Marta admits she’s facing a significant hurdle: the upcoming Christmas rush. With increased demand, she’s questioning whether a four-day week can sustain the bakery’s operations. “We’re managing, but I’m worried about the pressure,” she confessed. 

Marta’s Bakery is a real-world example of how businesses are experimenting with work-life balance. Will this bold move pay off, particularly during the busiest time of year? Stay tuned for updates!
