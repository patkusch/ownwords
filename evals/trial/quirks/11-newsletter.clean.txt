## Autumn Sale a Huge Success for Ashby Road Garden

Over 150 community members came to our Autumn Sale on October 18th to support the Ashby Road Garden. We raised **£1,340**. Thank you, everyone, for your generosity and support.

A huge thank you to our volunteers, particularly Ruth and Imran, who built six new raised beds. That’s significantly more growing space. We’re also really pleased with our brand-new water butt, now holding 1,000 litres of water for the plants.

But there’s a possible problem ahead. We’ve been told that the council may reduce our grant funding next year, with a decision expected in January. We’ll keep a close eye on it and look at all the options to keep the garden going.
