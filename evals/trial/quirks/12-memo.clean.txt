**MEMORANDUM**

**TO:** Head of IT Operations
**FROM:** [Your Name/Department]
**DATE:** 26 October 2026
**SUBJECT:** Recommendation: Trial of Remote First-Line IT Support

This memo recommends a 12-week trial of remote first-line IT support, to deal with increasing help-desk ticket volumes and improve response times.

Our current help-desk team of three handles 1,840 tickets per quarter. Password resets account for approximately 70% of requests. The result is significant wait times, which affect user productivity.

We propose a 12-week trial of a remote support model, starting January 11, 2027. A successful trial at the Leeds office in 2025 reduced average wait time from 22 minutes to 9, and we believe the same approach can significantly improve efficiency here.

The estimated cost of the trial, including software and training, is under £5,000.

We strongly recommend starting this trial to assess the feasibility and impact of remote first-line support in our environment. Success will be measured by reduced average ticket resolution time and overall user satisfaction.

As next steps, we will develop a detailed implementation plan and secure stakeholder approval for the start of the trial.
