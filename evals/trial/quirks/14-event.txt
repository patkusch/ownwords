**The Riverside Book Festival Returns**

The 7th annual Riverside Book Festival takes place in beautiful Shrewsbury form June 12th to 14th, 2026. Over the weekend we’re welcoming over 34 authors, including bestselling novelists Nadia Halabi and Tom Reece.

Come and find your next favourite read and meet your favourite authors.

Festival details:

* Dates: June 12th - 14th, 2026
* Location: Shrewsbury
* Authors: Nadia Halabi, Tom Reece & many more
* Tickets: Weekend pass – £45, Day ticket – £18, Under 16s free
* Saturday evening: a special talk starting at 19:30

Last year’s festival attracted over 2,600 book lovers.
