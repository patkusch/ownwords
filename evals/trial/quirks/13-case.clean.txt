## Dunmore Plumbing: A Cautious Embrace of Online Booking

**Case Study: March 2026**

Dunmore Plumbing is a family-run firm employing 11 people, run by owner Colm Dunmore. It introduced an online booking system on March 1st, 2026, to streamline its service scheduling and make booking more convenient for customers. Before that, Colm and his team relied solely on phone calls for appointment requests, which was proving increasingly inefficient.

Colm met the initial rollout with some skepticism and had concerns about the fees. The system went live anyway, giving customers 24/7 access to view available slots and book appointments.

Within the first month, the effect was noticeable. Missed calls fell from approximately 40 per week to 12. But no-shows stayed the same, which suggests the booking system hadn’t fundamentally changed customer behaviour.

Bookings outside standard office hours also rose, and now account for 28% of the total bookings. Colm remains cautiously optimistic. “I was sceptical and I am still not sure about the fees," he said. The system’s long-term value and potential return on investment still need to be assessed.

**Key Takeaway:** Dunmore Plumbing saw a clear improvement in how efficiently it manages appointments. But the owner's reservations show why a careful cost-benefit analysis matters when adopting new technology.
