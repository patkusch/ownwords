**Leeds Bakery Tries Four Days a Week. Is It Working?**

Marta Kowalczyk, owner of Marta’s Bakery in Leeds, took a gamble in February 2026: she switched her team to a four-day week. Starting her business on the 2nd, she initially employed 9 staff and saw a slight dip in sales of around 4% in the first month. Things quickly rebounded.

Marta’s also been tracking the wellbeing of her team. Two staff members have reported significantly improved sleep patterns.

“It’s been incredible to see the difference,” Marta told us. “The staff feel more energized and focused.”

But Marta admits she’s facing a significant hurdle: the upcoming Christmas rush. With increased demand, she’s questioning whether a four-day week can sustain the bakery’s operations. “We’re managing, but I’m worried about the pressure,” she confessed.

Marta’s Bakery is a real-world example of how businesses are experimenting with work-life balance. Will it pay off, particularly during the busiest time of year? Stay tuned for updates.
