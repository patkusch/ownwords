**Elm Park Primary Reading Programme – Interim Report (September 2025 - March 2026)**

This report gives the interim findings of the school-led reading programme run at Elm Park Primary between September 2025 and March 2026 for pupils in Years 3 and 4 (n=112). The programme cost £14 per pupil and focused on enhanced literacy support and targeted intervention strategies.

Initial results show promising progress across the cohort. Analysis indicates an average rise in reading age of 7 months, which reflects the impact of the focused support. But 18 pupils made no measurable progress.

Headteacher Joan Whitlock has noted “early signs are encouraging,” but also emphasized the requirement for continued data collection. She believes further analysis is crucial, particularly of the reasons behind the 18 pupils’ lack of progress.

We will continue to monitor pupil progress closely, using formative assessments and adapting our approach where necessary. A full evaluation will be carried out after the programme is completed in March 2026. It will look more closely at the factors influencing outcomes, and will allow a more comprehensive assessment of the programme’s overall effectiveness.
