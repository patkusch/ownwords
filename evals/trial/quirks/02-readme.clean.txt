## Tidepool - Fast Photo Folder Sync

Tidepool is a lightweight, open-source tool written in Go that syncs your photo folders. Version 2.3 was released on May 5th, 2026, with improved performance and reliability.

It saves you transferring photos between devices by hand. Tidepool does it for you and keeps your galleries consistent across all your locations.

**Key features**

* Fast: version 2.3 is approximately three times the speed of previous versions (based on author's internal testing).
* Successfully tested with over 40,000 files.
* Easy to configure and use.
* Written in Go, with performance and efficiency in mind.

Our testing shows significant speed improvements, but performance may vary depending on disk speed.

For detailed instructions and troubleshooting, see the documentation: [https://tidepool.example/docs](https://tidepool.example/docs)
