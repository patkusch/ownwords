# Tidepool

Tidepool keeps photo folders in sync across your devices, so you don't have to copy photos by hand. It is open source and written in Go.

## Version 2.3

Version 2.3 came out on 5 May 2026.

- It is about three times faster than earlier versions. That figure comes from my own tests, not an independent benchmark, and your results will depend on how fast your disks are.
- It has been tested on folders of more than 40,000 files.

## Setup and help

Setup instructions and troubleshooting are in the docs: https://tidepool.example/docs
