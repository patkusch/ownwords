Is the Oakline water filter safe for my well water?

Not on its own. The Oakline filter is not certified for well water, and it does not remove bacteria or nitrates, which are two of the main risks in a private well.

What it does do: in tests by Hartwell Labs in 2025, it removed at least 99% of lead and chlorine. It also improves taste and smell.

Before you use it on well water, have the water tested by a professional. The test will tell you what is in it. If it finds bacteria or nitrates, you need other treatment as well, and the Oakline filter will not make that water safe to drink.

One cartridge filters about 400 litres, which is around two months for most households.
