Elm Park Primary reading programme: interim report, September 2025 to March 2026

Elm Park Primary ran its own reading programme for Years 3 and 4 from September 2025 to March 2026. It covered 112 pupils and cost £14 per pupil.

On average, reading age rose by 7 months over the period. That average hides a gap: 18 pupils made no measurable progress, and we do not yet know why.

Headteacher Joan Whitlock said "early signs are encouraging", and added that the school needs to keep collecting data. She wants the 18 pupils who did not progress looked at in particular.

We will keep tracking pupils' progress and change the approach where it is not working. A full evaluation will follow now that the programme has finished. It will look more closely at what made the difference for the pupils who improved and what held the others back.
