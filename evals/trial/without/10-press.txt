Castell Museum's east wing reopens on 3 April 2027

The east wing of the Castell Museum will reopen on 3 April 2027 after 19 months of renovation. It has 14 new rooms.

The work cost €2.1 million, of which the regional council paid €600,000.

"We expect around 90,000 visitors in the first year," said the museum's director, Ines Moreau.

Entry will be free on the opening weekend.

Opening hours and events are at https://castellmuseum.example.
