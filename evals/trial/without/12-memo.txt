MEMO

To: Head of IT Operations
Date: 26 October 2026
Subject: Proposal to trial remote first-line IT support

I recommend a 12-week trial of remote first-line support, starting on 11 January 2027.

Why
The help desk has three people and handles 1,840 tickets a quarter. About 70% of those are password resets. Users wait too long for simple fixes.

Evidence
The Leeds office ran a similar trial in 2025. Average wait time fell from 22 minutes to 9.

Cost
Under £5,000, including software and training.

How we will judge it
Two measures: the average time to resolve a ticket, and what users say about the service.

Next step
If you agree, I will write up a detailed plan for approval before the start date.
