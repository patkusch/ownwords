A Leeds bakery tries the four-day week

On 2 February 2026, Marta Kowalczyk moved the nine staff at Marta's Bakery in Leeds to a four-day week.

Sales fell by about 4% in the first month, then recovered.

Marta has also been keeping an eye on how her staff are doing. Two of them say they are sleeping better. She says the team seems to have more energy and concentrates better at work.

The hard test is still ahead. Christmas is the bakery's busiest time, and Marta does not know whether four days will be enough to cover it. They are managing for now, she says, but the pressure worries her.

So it is too early to call this a success. One small bakery, one dip in sales that passed, two people sleeping better, and a Christmas rush still to come. We will report back in the new year.
