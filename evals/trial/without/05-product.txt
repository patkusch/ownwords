Fennel 3 bicycle light

The Fennel 3 is a front light that puts out 800 lumens, enough for drivers and other riders to see you well before they reach you.

The battery lasts up to 6 hours on the low setting and recharges over USB-C in 2 hours. The light weighs 96 grams.

It is rated IPX6, which means it keeps working in heavy rain and road spray.

$49, with a 2-year warranty.
