Riverside Book Festival, Shrewsbury, 12 to 14 June 2026

The Riverside Book Festival is back for its seventh year, with 34 authors over three days. Among them are the novelists Nadia Halabi and Tom Reece.

Tickets
Weekend pass: £45
Day ticket: £18
Under 16s: free

On Saturday evening there is a special talk at 19:30.

Last year 2,600 people came.
