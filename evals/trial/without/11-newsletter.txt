Autumn Sale raises £1,340

Thank you to everyone who came to the Autumn Sale on 18 October. More than 150 of you turned up, and together we raised £1,340 for Ashby Road Garden.

Special thanks to Ruth and Imran, who built six new raised beds, so we now have a good deal more room to grow. We also have a new water butt that holds 1,000 litres.

One piece of less good news. The council has told us it may cut our grant next year. It is due to decide in January. We will tell you as soon as we hear, and in the meantime we are looking at other ways to keep the garden going.
