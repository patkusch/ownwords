Dunmore Plumbing: one month of online booking

Dunmore Plumbing is a family firm of 11 people, run by its owner, Colm Dunmore. Until this year, customers could only book by phone. On 1 March 2026 the firm switched on an online booking system, which lets customers see free slots and book at any hour.

Colm was not keen. His worry was the fees.

After the first month, three things were clear.

Missed calls fell from about 40 a week to 12.

28% of bookings now come in outside office hours.

No-shows did not change. Customers who book online fail to turn up just as often as those who phoned.

Colm has not made up his mind. "I was sceptical and I am still not sure about the fees," he says.

What this shows: the system did what it promised on missed calls and out-of-hours bookings. Whether that is worth the fees is a sum the firm still has to do.
