## Autumn Sale a Huge Success for Ashby Road Garden!

Over 150 community members came to our Autumn Sale on October 18th to support the Ashby Road Garden. We raised **£1,340**, thanks to everyone’s generosity and support.

A huge thank you to our volunteers, particularly Ruth and Imran, who worked tirelessly to build six new raised beds. That gives us significantly more growing space. We’re also thrilled with our brand-new water butt, now holding 1,000 litres of water.

But there’s a possible problem. We’ve been told that the council may reduce our grant funding next year, with a decision expected in January. We’ll keep a close eye on it and explore all options to keep the garden flourishing.
