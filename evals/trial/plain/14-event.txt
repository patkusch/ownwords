**The Riverside Book Festival Returns!**

The 7th annual Riverside Book Festival takes place in Shrewsbury from June 12th to 14th, 2026. We’re welcoming over 34 authors, including bestselling novelists Nadia Halabi and Tom Reece, and many more.

Come and find your next favourite read and meet your favourite authors.

Tickets:

* Weekend pass – £45
* Day ticket – £18
* Under 16s free

Don't miss our special Saturday evening talk, starting at 19:30.

Last year’s festival attracted over 2,600 book lovers.
