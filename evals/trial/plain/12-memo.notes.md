    **MEMORANDUM**

    **TO:** Head of IT Operations
    **FROM:** [Your Name/Department]
    **DATE:** 26 October 2026
    **SUBJECT:** Recommendation: Trial of Remote First-Line IT Support

    **Executive Summary:** This memo recommends a 12-week trial of remote first-line IT support, to deal with rising help-desk ticket volumes and improve response times.

    **Background:** Our help-desk team of three handles 1,840 tickets per quarter. Approximately 70% of requests are password resets. This means significant wait times, which hurt user productivity.

    **Proposed Solution:** We propose a 12-week trial of a remote support model, starting January 11, 2027. A trial at the Leeds office in 2025 was successful: it cut average wait time from 22 minutes to 9. We believe this approach can significantly improve efficiency.

    **Financial Considerations:** The estimated cost of this trial, including software and training, is under £5,000.

    **Recommendation:** We strongly recommend starting this trial to assess whether remote first-line support is feasible in our environment and what impact it has. Success will be measured by reduced average ticket resolution time and overall user satisfaction.

    **Next Steps:** We will write a detailed plan for carrying out the trial and get stakeholder approval to start it.

## 3. What changed and why

- Plain words: "starting" for "commencing" and "initiating", "cut" for "reduced", dropped "utilizing" and "Leveraging learnings from".
- The Leeds result now stands as its own sentence, so the two figures are easy to find.
- Split the background sentence so the ticket count and the password share each get one.
- "Develop a detailed implementation plan and secure stakeholder approval for the trial commencement" became "write a detailed plan for carrying out the trial and get stakeholder approval to start it".
- Kept the memo layout and the labelled sections. No contractions.
