Subject: Canal Street Bridge Repair Project Update – October 26, 2023

Dear Stakeholders,

Here is a progress report on the Canal Street Bridge repair project. As of today, October 26th, the project is 62% complete. But we’ve had an unforeseen delay that affects the originally scheduled completion date of September 30th, 2026.

We now expect to finish in late November. The main cause is a critical delay in the delivery of steel components: the shipment arrived five weeks behind schedule.

The project budget stands at £3.4 million, and we have spent £2.3 million so far. We’re watching material costs closely and working with suppliers to limit further disruption.

Our engineering team, led by Fatima Noor, is working to speed up the remaining tasks. We’ve made excellent progress, but heavy rainfall could potentially add another week of delay. We’re watching the weather forecasts closely.

There have been no reported injuries on site, and safety remains our top priority. We’ll keep sending regular updates and will tell you immediately about any significant developments.
