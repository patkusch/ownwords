    ## Dunmore Plumbing: A Cautious Embrace of Online Booking

    **Case Study: March 2026**

    Dunmore Plumbing is a family-run firm that employs 11 people and is run by its owner, Colm Dunmore. On March 1st, 2026, it started using an online booking system, aiming to simplify scheduling and make booking more convenient for customers. Before this, Colm and his team relied solely on phone calls for appointment requests, a process that was proving increasingly inefficient.

    Colm met the rollout with some scepticism and had concerns about the fees. The system went live anyway, giving customers 24/7 access to view available slots and book appointments.

    The effect showed within the first month. Missed calls fell from approximately 40 per week to 12. But no-shows stayed the same, which suggests the booking system hadn’t fundamentally changed customer behaviour.

    Perhaps the most significant change was the rise in bookings outside standard office hours, which now account for 28% of the total bookings. Colm remains cautiously optimistic. “I was sceptical and I am still not sure about the fees," he says.

    **Key Takeaway:** Dunmore Plumbing saw a clear improvement in how it manages appointments. But the owner's initial reservations show why a careful cost-benefit analysis matters when adopting new technology.

## 3. What changed and why

- Cut the emphasis words: "Crucially", "plummeted", "a significantly reduced 12", "a substantial 28%", "a key observation was that". The numbers do the work.
- Cut the comment tacked on after the quotation: "highlighting the ongoing need to assess the system's long-term value and potential for return on investment".
- Split the 37-word opening sentence.
- Plain words: "people" for "individuals", "started using" for "implemented", "simplify" for "streamline", "before" for "prior to", "show why ... matters" for "underscore the importance of".
- "The system was activated" became "the system went live". The text does not say who switched it on, so nobody is named.

## 5. Left alone on purpose, and questions

- The quotation is word for word, including its closing mark, which is a straight " where the opening one is curly. I added "he says" after it.
- "Approximately 40", "suggesting", "Perhaps the most significant change" and "cautiously optimistic" all keep their strength.
- The original spells it "skepticism" once and "sceptical" in the quotation. I used the quotation's spelling throughout.
- Question: "no-shows remained consistent" has no figure. Do you have one? And what are the fees Colm is unsure about?
