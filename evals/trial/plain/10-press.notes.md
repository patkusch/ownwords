## 3. What changed and why

- Cut "highly anticipated" and "expressed excitement about the reopening, stating": the second quotation already says she is thrilled, so "said" is enough.
- "Announced the reopening of its east wing on April 3rd, 2027" could be read as the announcement date; it now says the wing "will reopen on" that date.
- "Marks a significant expansion of" became "significantly expands"; "has resulted in" became "has added"; "comprehensive" became "full"; "following" became "after".
- No contractions or loose sentences added: it goes out under the museum's name.
