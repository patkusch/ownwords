## Tidepool - Fast Photo Folder Sync

**Tidepool** is a lightweight, open-source tool written in Go that syncs your photo folders. It saves you moving photos between devices by hand. It keeps your galleries the same across all your locations.

Version 2.3 was released on May 5th, 2026, with better performance and reliability.

## Features

* Version 2.3 is approximately three times as fast as previous versions (based on author's internal testing).
* Tested successfully with over 40,000 files.
* Easy to configure and use.

Our testing shows significant speed improvements, but performance may vary depending on disk speed.
