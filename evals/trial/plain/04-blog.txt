**Leeds Bakery Takes a Bold Step: Four Days a Week – Is It Working?**

Marta Kowalczyk, owner of Marta’s Bakery in Leeds, took a gamble in February 2026: she switched her team to a four-day week. Starting her business on the 2nd, she initially employed 9 staff and saw a slight dip in sales of around 4% in the first month. Thankfully, things quickly rebounded.

Marta’s also been tracking the wellbeing of her team. Two staff members have reported significantly improved sleep patterns. That’s a huge benefit for anyone working long hours.

“It’s been incredible to see the difference,” Marta told us. “The staff feel more energized and focused.”

But Marta admits she’s facing a significant hurdle: the upcoming Christmas rush. With increased demand, she’s questioning whether the bakery can keep running on a four-day week. “We’re managing, but I’m worried about the pressure,” she said.

Marta’s Bakery is a real-world example of how businesses are experimenting with work-life balance. Will the move pay off, particularly during the busiest time of year? Stay tuned for updates.
