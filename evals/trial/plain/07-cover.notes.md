- Plain words: "have" for "possess", "led" for "spearheaded", "methods" for "methodologies", "too" for "Furthermore".
- Cut filler: "enthusiastic", "successfully", "thrive in this role and contribute significantly to your team", "dedication to efficient execution".
- A few contractions only; it is still a formal letter.
