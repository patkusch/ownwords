**Elm Park Primary Reading Programme – Interim Report (September 2025 - March 2026)**

This report gives the interim findings of the school-led reading programme run at Elm Park Primary between September 2025 and March 2026 for Years 3 and 4 pupils (n=112). The programme cost £14 per pupil. It focused on enhanced literacy support and targeted intervention strategies.

Initial results show promising progress across the cohort. Analysis indicates an average rise in reading age of 7 months. However, 18 pupils made no measurable progress.

Headteacher Joan Whitlock has noted “early signs are encouraging,” but also stressed the need to keep collecting data. She believes further analysis is crucial, particularly of the reasons the 18 pupils did not progress.

We will keep monitoring pupil progress closely, using formative assessments and adapting our approach where necessary. A full evaluation will follow once the programme is complete in March 2026. It will look more deeply at the factors influencing outcomes, and allow a more comprehensive assessment of the programme’s overall effectiveness.
