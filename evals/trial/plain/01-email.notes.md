## 3. What changed and why

- Opened with the news itself; "I'm writing to provide an update following the recent..." said nothing.
- Plain words: "had" for "experienced", "make sure" for "ensure", "after" for "following", "waiting for" for "awaiting".
- "Exploring the possibility of a partial refund" became "looking into a possible partial refund". Still only a possibility, still not confirmed.
- Cut "to compensate for the inconvenience and disruption caused" down to "to make up for the disruption" (doubled words).
- Closing line shortened; "please don't hesitate" is a stock phrase.

## 5. Left alone on purpose, and questions

- Left alone: subject line, greeting, sign-off, every figure, the address.
- "Complete lack of heating" is now "had no heating". My first two tries ("no heating at all", "completely without heating") were flagged by the check as added certainty although the original says "complete". A false alarm, but I reworded instead of waiving.
- Question: "refund to the service charge" - I wrote "refund of the service charge". Is that what is meant, or a credit against it?

This rewrite makes the text read better. It is not meant to pass an AI detector, and I am not claiming it will.
