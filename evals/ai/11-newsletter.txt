## Autumn Sale a Huge Success for Ashby Road Garden!

A fantastic turnout at our Autumn Sale on October 18th saw over 150 community members come together to support the Ashby Road Garden! We raised a phenomenal **£1,340**, thanks to everyone’s generosity and support – a truly incredible achievement! 

A huge thank you to our dedicated volunteers, particularly Ruth and Imran, who worked tirelessly to build six new raised beds, significantly expanding our growing space. We’re also thrilled with our brand-new water butt, now holding a whopping 1,000 litres of water – perfect for keeping our plants thriving!

However, we face a potential challenge. We’ve been informed that the council may reduce our grant funding next year, with a decision expected in January. We’ll be keeping a close eye on developments and exploring all options to ensure the garden continues to flourish. 

Let’s keep the momentum going!
