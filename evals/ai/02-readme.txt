## Tidepool - Fast Photo Folder Sync

**Tidepool** is a lightweight, open-source tool written in Go designed to efficiently synchronize your photo folders. Version 2.3 was released on May 5th, 2026, offering improved performance and reliability.

Tired of manually transferring photos between devices? Tidepool automates this process, keeping your galleries consistent across all your locations. 

**Key Features:**

* **Fast Synchronization:**  Version 2.3 boasts approximately three times the speed of previous versions (based on author's internal testing).
* **Robust Testing:**  Successfully tested with over 40,000 files.
* **Simple Setup:**  Easy to configure and use.
* **Go-Based:**  Built with performance and efficiency in mind.

**Important Note:** While our testing shows significant speed improvements, performance may vary depending on disk speed.
