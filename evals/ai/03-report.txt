**Elm Park Primary Reading Programme – Interim Report (September 2025 - March 2026)**

This report outlines the interim findings of the school-led reading programme implemented at Elm Park Primary between September 2025 and March 2026, targeting Years 3 and 4 pupils (n=112). Initial results demonstrate promising progress across the cohort.  Analysis indicates an average rise in reading age of 7 months, reflecting the impact of the focused support. However, it’s important to acknowledge that 18 pupils made no measurable progress, highlighting the need for a nuanced understanding of individual learning needs. 

Headteacher Joan Whitlock has noted “early signs are encouraging,” but also emphasized the requirement for continued data collection.  She believes further analysis, particularly focusing on the reasons behind the 18 pupils’ lack of progress, is crucial. 

Moving forward, we will continue to monitor pupil progress closely, utilising formative assessments and adapting our approach where necessary.  A full evaluation, incorporating a deeper dive into the factors influencing outcomes, will be conducted following the completion of the programme in March 2026, allowing for a more comprehensive assessment of its overall effectiveness.
